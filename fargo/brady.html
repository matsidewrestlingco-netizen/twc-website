Team Pennsylvania · Returning Qualifier

I've earned the opportunity to represent Team Pennsylvania at the USMC Junior Nationals in Fargo, North Dakota — again.

My name is Brady Rohaly. This is a very big honor for me: it puts me in front of the eyes of many college coaches and gives me the chance to perform in front of them. As I head into my senior year of high school, this could lead to opportunities that may not have been available before.

Fargo is the biggest wrestling tournament for high schoolers, represented by almost every state. Before the tournament, Team Pennsylvania will hold training camp at Clarion, and we'll leave from there for Fargo. Including gear, camp, travel, lodging, and the entry fee, the total comes to around $1,200.

This is a lot on my family — especially after all they already do for me — so I'm trying to raise as much as I can on my own. — B. Rohaly · 138 GR+FS

Thank you for your consideration.

§ 04 — Background

Why it matters.

Fargo · Team Pennsylvania
Fargo, ND · July 13–15

The largest age-group tournament in the country.

The USA Wrestling 16U & Junior National Championships — known to the wrestling world simply as "Fargo" — is where every state sends its top qualifying athletes. All-American honors (top 8) from Fargo are among the most coveted credentials in high school wrestling and weigh heavily in college recruiting.
